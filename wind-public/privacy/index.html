Kilat privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026

Kilat shows weather and air quality in Singapore. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect or sell personal data.

Your location

If you allow it, Kilat uses your location on your iPhone to choose the nearest weather stations and forecast area. Your location is not sent to us or stored on any server. Without location access, Kilat shows central Singapore.

What leaves your iPhone

To show data, Kilat downloads public weather, air-quality and map information from these services:

  • Singapore's National Environment Agency, through data.gov.sg
  • Kilat's own data service (Cloudflare), which republishes public haze, hotspot, regional air and wind data
  • Mapbox, for map tiles

These requests do not include your location, name or any identifier from us. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address and basic device information to the service that answers them, which handles it under its own privacy policy. Map requests show which area of the map you are viewing. Kilat turns off Mapbox's optional usage telemetry.

What stays on your iPhone

Kilat keeps recent data and your settings (such as the selected lens, layers and air index) on your device so it opens quickly. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Kilat is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
